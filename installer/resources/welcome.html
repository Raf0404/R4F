Welcome to R4F

Multichannel audio for macOS: from your DAW to several audio interfaces, and from one app to another.

This installs:

  • R4F BUS — a 256-channel virtual device your DAW plays into;
  • R4F BOX — a 256-channel virtual device for passing audio between apps;
  • R4F — the menu bar app that routes the channels (in Applications);
  • R4F Out — a Max for Live device for Ableton Live (only if Live is installed).

Quit your DAW before continuing: at the end of the installation CoreAudio restarts and the audio of every app stops for a few seconds.

Older versions (LiveBus) are removed. Your existing configuration is kept.
