R4F is installed

R4F is now open: you'll find its icon in the menu bar, top right.

  1. When macOS asks, allow microphone access: R4F needs it to read R4F BUS.
  2. In your DAW, choose R4F BUS as the audio output device.
  3. From the R4F menu, open Configure R4F BUS… and decide where each channel goes.
  4. To pass audio between apps, use R4F BOX as the output in one and as the input in the other.

Full guide: github.com/Raf0404/R4F
